# Project Overview

## What this project is

A Power BI dashboard that monitors a company's financial health at transaction level: revenue, cost of goods sold, operating expenses, gross and net profit, profit margin, and performance against budget — sliced by region, department, product category, customer segment, and sales channel.

## Why financial KPI monitoring and profitability analysis matter

A business can have strong sales and still lose money if costs aren't tracked alongside revenue. Financial KPI dashboards exist to close that gap:

- **Revenue tells you activity; profit tells you health.** A region can top the revenue chart and still have the thinnest margin.
- **Budget variance catches drift early.** Spotting a 10% overspend in Q1 is cheap to fix; spotting it in Q4 is not.
- **Trend visibility supports forecasting.** Leadership needs to see whether margin is expanding or compressing month over month, not just the current snapshot.
- **Channel and segment breakdowns guide resource allocation.** Knowing which sales channel or customer segment is most profitable — not just highest-volume — changes where a company invests next.

## How companies use dashboards like this one

Finance and business teams typically review a dashboard like this on a monthly or quarterly cadence: pulling actuals from ERP/accounting systems, comparing them to budget, and flagging categories or regions that need attention before the next planning cycle. The dashboard becomes the shared source of truth in budget review meetings, board reporting, and departmental performance discussions.

## Job roles that use this kind of tool

- **Finance Analyst** — monitors revenue, cost, and margin trends
- **FP&A (Financial Planning & Analysis) Analyst** — owns budget vs. actual tracking and forecasting
- **Business Analyst** — builds and maintains the reporting layer for stakeholders
- **Management Consultant** — uses profitability breakdowns to recommend strategic changes
- **Operations/Business Unit Manager** — tracks their own department or region's contribution to profit

## How MBA and business-analytics students benefit

This project gives a portfolio piece that speaks the language recruiters in finance and BI roles expect: gross/net profit, margin, budget variance, and channel profitability — not just generic "sales dashboard" visuals. It also forces practice with the full BI workflow (data cleaning → modeling → DAX → storytelling), which is what's actually tested in BA/FP&A interviews.
